# Changelog

## 0.2.1

- The takeover now uses the sidebar cat instead of side-on runners: half the
  cats bounce up and down along the bottom, the rest leap onto the screen, dig
  their claws in and slowly slide down, leaving scratch marks that fade away a few seconds later.

## 0.2.0

- Session-end alerts now take over the editor: a herd of cats runs around the
  screen, bounces off the edges and pounces at the mouse pointer.
- "5 more minutes" returns to the session that just ended, without counting it
  twice.
- New settings: `pomocat.alertStyle`, `pomocat.alertCats`, `pomocat.snoozeMinutes`
  (replaces `pomocat.notify`).
- New command: `PomoCat: Unleash the Cats`.

## 0.1.0

- First release: pomodoro timer with focus / short break / long break cycle.
- Animated black ragdoll cat that reacts to each phase, and purrs when petted.
- Status bar timer, sidebar panel, commands and settings.
